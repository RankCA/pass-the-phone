Pass the Phone

Party games for one phone and a room full of people. Pick a game and pass it around.

Who's playing?

No names yet

    Add everyone once and every game starts with these names. They stay on this phone.

    Fast Money

    The host reads survey questions while everyone else wears headphones. Match the board to score.

    • 2 to 8 players
    • One host

    Impostor

    Everyone sees the secret word except the impostor. Give one-word clues and find who is faking it.

    • 3 to 12 players
    • Pass it around

    Tune In

    One player sees a hidden target on a dial and gives a clue. Everyone else turns the dial to find it.

    • 2 to 12 players
    • Teams or together

    Short Fuse

    Say a word that fits, then hand off the bomb. Whoever is holding it when it blows loses a life.

    • 2 to 12 players
    • Pass it fast

    Forehead

    Hold the phone on your forehead while friends give clues. Tilt down when you get it, up to pass.

    • 2 or more
    • Phone on your head

    Doodle Chain

    Draw a prompt, guess the drawing, draw the guess. Then watch how far it drifted.

    • 3 to 12 players
    • Draw and guess

    Copycats

    Everyone writes a one-word clue for the guesser. If two clues match, both get thrown out.

    • 3 to 10 players
    • Team up

    Most Likely To

    Who is most likely to forget their own birthday? Point on three or vote in secret, then read the yearbook.

    • 3 to 12 players
    • Vote on each other

    Every game runs on one phone. No accounts, nothing to install.
